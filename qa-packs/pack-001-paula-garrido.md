## Q1. Trauma and ADHD can look alike from the outside. How do you tell them apart?

**Draft answer**

> Both can produce difficulty concentrating, restlessness, emotional reactivity and broken
> sleep, so the surface presentation overlaps a great deal. Developmental history usually
> does more work than a symptom checklist: ADHD traits tend to show up early and across
> different settings, while trauma responses more often track a particular onset or
> context. They also co-occur often enough that it is frequently not an either/or question.

**CHECK:** Is developmental history the discriminator you would actually lead with, or
would you start somewhere else? And is "co-occur often enough" fair, or does it understate
how common the overlap is?

## Q2. What do you wish people brought to a first ADHD appointment?

**Draft answer**

> Examples rather than adjectives. "I'm disorganised" is hard to work with; "I missed three
> appointments this month and paid rent late twice" is not. Specific, recent, dated
> examples let a first session get somewhere instead of spending it establishing the basics.
> Old school reports help too, if they still exist somewhere.

**CHECK:** Is there something more useful people could bring that we have missed — a
collateral history from a partner or parent, previous assessments, a list of what has
already been tried?

## Q3. What pattern comes up most when ADHD affects a relationship?

**Draft answer**

> The drift into a parent-child dynamic. One partner gradually takes over the admin —
> appointments, bills, remembering things — and the other starts to feel managed rather
> than partnered. Resentment builds on both sides, usually quietly. Naming it as a problem
> with the system the couple has fallen into, rather than a fault in either person, tends
> to help more than a better chore roster does.

**CHECK:** This one is closest to clinical opinion rather than established fact, so it is
the most likely to need rewriting in your own words. Also: is it fair to present this as
the *most* common pattern, or should it be softened to *a* common one?
