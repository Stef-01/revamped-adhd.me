Find your clinician

The wait was never the care.

For years, an ADHD assessment meant a long, costly wait for a psychiatrist. The old route ran through a queue with no visible end and a cost most people could not plan for, paid twice: in time, and then again at the door. ADHD care in Australia asked for stamina rather than need. The front door was made for somebody with more time and money than most.

Finding a GP was its own search: one who knows how ADHD presents, including in adults who have compensated for years, and who will listen.

ADHD is not a sideline here. The GPs listed do this work regularly. You should not have to argue your case. Running late, losing the paperwork, or not being able to compress twenty years into five minutes is not counted against you; it is part of what you came to discuss. Follow-ups are with the doctor who already knows your history.

Find your clinician and book. That’s it.

Diagnosis, then a life that fits the brain you have.

The same wiring that makes the ordinary hard makes other things easy: quick connections, deep focus on what matters, energy when it counts. Care here is built to let that show.

  1. 1

    Diagnosis

  2. 2

    Ongoing care

    Diagnosis is the start. Medication where it helps, therapy, sleep, health and habits looked at together, with the clinician who knows your history. Care that is about getting more out of life, not just getting through it.
