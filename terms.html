Terms of use

What ADHDme is and is not, what you agree to by using it, and where responsibility sits.

The short version

ADHDme lists GPs and what each of them says about their own work, so you can read them and choose. It is not a medical service, it does not give medical advice, and using it creates no relationship between you and any doctor; that begins when you book and attend an appointment. By using the site you accept these terms and the privacy policy.

What the service is, and is not

Each GP is shown with what they themselves declare about their own work. We do not write it for them and we do not add an assessment of our own. Nothing on this site is a judgement about you or your health. Whether any appointment, assessment or care is right for you is a conversation with a doctor, not with a website. Booking is done on Healthengine or with the practice, not here.

The service is not for emergencies. If you or someone else is in immediate danger, call 000. If you want to talk to someone about how you are feeling, at any hour, Lifeline is 13 11 14.

Who is responsible for your care

Your practice provides your care. ADHDme is a listing that shows you GPs and hands you to the practice’s own booking page. It does not provide treatment, does not give clinical advice, and is not part of your care team. Anything about your health goes to your practice, not to us.

How the order of GPs is decided

The list is in roster order. It is not ranked and not personalised. No GP can pay to rank higher, and listing a clinician is not an endorsement or a statement about quality.

Clinician information

Everything shown about a clinician is their own declaration or their practice’s published information. If you believe something shown is wrong, tell us and we will check it with the clinician and correct it; a clinician can ask at any time for their listing to be corrected or removed.

Bookings and Healthengine

Booking happens on Healthengine, under Healthengine’s own terms and privacy policy. Your appointment, its time, fees and everything about it, is between you and the practice. ADHDme is not a party to the booking, does not see it, and does not set or receive any part of any fee.

Using the site fairly

Use the site lawfully and as a person looking for care, or a clinician engaging with their own listing. Do not misrepresent who you are when you write to us, attempt to interfere with the service, or harvest its content by automated bulk access.

Content and ownership

The site’s text, design and software are ours or licensed to us. What a clinician declares about their own work remains theirs, shown here with their consent. Nothing you send us is used for any purpose beyond the one it was given for; see the privacy policy.

The service while it is early

The product is under active development: features change, listings are few, and the service may be unavailable at times without notice. We work to keep everything shown accurate to what clinicians have declared, and we say so plainly when something is a demonstration or an illustration.

Australian Consumer Law

Nothing in these terms takes away any right or remedy you have under the Australian Consumer Law or any other law that cannot be excluded. To the extent the law allows, our responsibility for a failure of the service is limited to resupplying it, and we are not responsible for the acts of the third parties named here, Healthengine, or the practice you book with, whose services are governed by their own terms.

Governing law, changes and contact

These terms are governed by the law of New South Wales, Australia. If they change, the date and the change will be stated here. They are in force as published. Questions go to info@adhdme.au. If any part of these terms cannot be enforced, the rest still applies.
