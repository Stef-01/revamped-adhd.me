Last updated 26 September 2026

These terms apply when you use the ADHDme website. By using the site, you agree to them and to our privacy policy.

2. What ADHDme is

ADHDme is a directory of ADHD clinicians in Australia, including GPs, psychologists, allied health clinicians and coaches. We don’t provide health care or medical advice, and using the site doesn’t create a relationship between you and any clinician. That starts when you book with a practice.

ADHDme is not for emergencies. If you or someone else is in immediate danger, call 000. For support at any hour, call Lifeline on 13 11 14.

3. Clinician information

Profiles are based on what each clinician or their practice publishes about their own work. Clinicians are not ranked, no clinician can pay to appear higher, and a listing is not an endorsement.

If something on a profile looks wrong, email us and we will check it with the clinician. A clinician can ask us to correct or remove their listing at any time.

4. Booking with a practice

Booking links take you to the practice’s booking system or website. Your appointment, including its time and fees, is between you and the practice, under that site’s terms and privacy policy. ADHDme is not a party to the booking and doesn’t take any part of the fee.
