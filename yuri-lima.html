About

With a lifelong passion for movement and sports, Yuri’s approach combines clinical expertise in orthopaedic and sports rehabilitation and patient-centred care to help clients return to their optimal level of function and performance.

Read more

He believes in empowering individuals through education and active involvement in their recovery process. He is also committed to advancing the field of physiotherapy by holding a Master in Rehabilitation Sciences and a PhD where he investigated ACL injuries in Athletes.
