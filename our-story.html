Why we exist

ADHDme was founded by two medical trainees with ADHD who experienced care very differently.

One found he functioned best with a largely non-medication approach.

The other saw medication as an important part of treatment.

But both encountered the same gap: too many people with ADHD felt unheard, or left appointments with a prescription but without the practical strategies, broader support or specialist expertise they needed to thrive.

They both kept thinking the same thing: there has to be more to it than this.
  1. 01

    Someone who asks about your life, not just your symptoms.

  2. 02

    An appointment you don’t walk out of with a script and nothing else.

  3. 03

    Help with the parts medication doesn’t reach: sleep, food, exercise, work, relationships, and the mornings that fall apart anyway.

  4. 04

    People to see next who already understand ADHD, whether that’s a psychologist, an occupational therapist, an exercise physiologist or a dietitian, so you’re not explaining yourself from the beginning every time.

  5. 05

    And the research put in plain words, so you can actually use it.

That is what ADHDme is for.If you’ve spent years being told to try harder, you already know effort was never the problem. We help you work out what really gets in your way, build something practical around it, and spend more of your energy on what your brain is good at.

Managing symptoms is where care starts, not where it ends. You should come away understanding yourself better, with the right people around you, and with more of your life going the way you want it to.

Care that meets you where you are.

We’re building a network of ADHD-focused clinicians city by city, starting with the east coast.
