Why we exist

Built by two medical trainees who have ADHD.

One does best mostly without medication.

The other relies on it.

Both saw the same problem: people left appointments with a script and no plan.

There has to be more to it than this.
  1. 01

    Someone who asks about your life as well as your symptoms.

  2. 02

    A plan to go with your script.

  3. 03

    Help with the things medication doesn’t cover, like sleep, food, work and relationships.

  4. 04

    The next clinician you see already understands ADHD, so you don’t have to start over.

  5. 05

    The research, explained in plain English.

You were never lazy.Years of being told to “try harder” didn’t help, because effort was never the problem. We work out what gets in your way and plan around it.

Care starts with your symptoms and goes on from there.

Where we are, and where we’re going.

Clinicians in Sydney, Brisbane, the Gold Coast, Perth and the Snowy Mountains today, with telehealth Australia-wide. More cities follow as clinicians join.
