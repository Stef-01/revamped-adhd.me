Privacy policy

What ADHDme holds, what it never holds, and the choices you keep.

The short version

Reading these pages tells us nothing about you. There is nothing on them to fill in: the list of GPs and each doctor's own page are built ahead of time and served the same way to everybody. The only information we hold is what you deliberately hand over: an email if you write to us. Everything below is the detail of those two sentences.

The network, and what it does not hold

The doctor pages hold nothing at all. There is nothing to type on them: the list of GPs and each doctor's own page are built ahead of time and served as they are, the same for everybody, so reading them tells us nothing about you beyond the ordinary web request your browser makes for any page.

Reading the site, and children

You can read every page on this site without telling us who you are. There is no account, no sign-in, and nothing to type in order to see a GP's page.

Some people are looking on behalf of a child or teenager in their care. Nothing on these pages asks for anything about them, so nothing about them reaches us.

What we collect today, the complete list

If you email us, we hold what you sent: your address and your message, used only to reply to you. That is the whole of it: no accounts, no profiles, no forms, and nothing collected from the pages you browse.

Access, correction and deletion

If you have written to us, email us and we will show you what we hold, correct it, or delete it. You do not need to be anyone's patient to ask.

Retention

Email correspondence is kept while a conversation is open or until the person asks for it to be removed, whichever comes first.

Who is responsible for your care

Your practice provides your care. ADHDme is a listing that shows you GPs and hands you to the practice's own booking page. It does not provide treatment, does not give clinical advice, and is not part of your care team. Anything about your health goes to your practice, not to us.

Automated decision-making

Nothing on this site decides anything about you. The listing shows every GP in roster order: it is not ranked, not personalised, and there is no history or profile of you for it to read. Our automated-decisions statement describes what is automated, what never is, and the human controls in force.

How information is held

The site is served by GitHub Pages, and its files may be stored on infrastructure in the United States. It holds no database: there is nothing here to hold. Transport is encrypted.

If something goes wrong

If we suspect information we hold has been lost or accessed without authority, we will assess it promptly, tell the people affected what happened and what we are doing about it, and notify the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner where the Notifiable Data Breaches scheme requires it.

Cookies and local storage

The site sets no advertising cookies. One value is kept in your browser's own storage: a record that you have seen and agreed to this policy, which never leaves your device.

Page visits are not counted today. If we turn on a visit counter, it will count without cookies and without advertising signals, and this section will say so on the day it starts. When you follow a booking link to Healthengine, we count that the link was used, not who used it, and from the moment their page opens, Healthengine's own privacy policy governs what you enter there. How that count works, and where it can be read, is set out on the measurement page.

Complaints

If you think we have mishandled your information, contact us first and we will respond within two business days. If you are not satisfied with the outcome, you can complain to the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (oaic.gov.au).

Contact and changes

Questions about this policy go to info@adhdme.au. If this policy changes, the date and the change will be stated here. It is in force as published. No patient data is processed by this site.
