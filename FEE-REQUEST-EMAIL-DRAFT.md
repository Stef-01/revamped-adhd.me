If you are comfortable with it, we would like to show either your standard session fee or a "from" price,

1. the fee or from-price you are happy to publish, and what it covers (for example, a 50-minute session); or
2. "keep it as it is".

One more question while we are here: when someone sends you an enquiry from our site, roughly how soon
should they expect a reply? We would like to tell them, and we will not guess on your behalf.
