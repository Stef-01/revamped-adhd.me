## Platform

web

## Stack

static HTML/CSS with a small amount of vanilla JS, no build step, deployed from `main` on GitHub Pages. (Inherited from the repository; the founder's placeholder README set "no build, no dependencies" as the standard.)

## Users

Adults in Australia (18 and over) who think they may have ADHD and want an assessment, usually reading on a phone, often tired, uncertain or overwhelmed, and often after years of being told to wait or to argue their case. A second reader is the organisation that refers people on (NGOs, community services); a third is a GP who might work with the service. This page serves the first.

## Product Purpose

ADHDme delivers the whole ADHD pathway for adults: assessment, diagnosis and, where appropriate, the start of treatment, then ongoing review, with AHPRA-registered clinicians, online or in person. Under 18, it refers on to an appropriate specialist. Success is a visitor understanding in seconds what the service is, who it is for and what the pathway involves, and choosing to register interest.

*Model note (inferred from the founder's direction on 2026-09-15: "we are not a membership but an ADHD diagnosis model"):* the sources describe three models, a free GP finder (the live `adhd.me` app), a healthcare provider (the 2026-08/09 film briefs), and a membership (the discarded Stitch draft). This page follows the provider model. The finder's "not a medical service" line and the draft's "no assessment is offered here" footer therefore do not apply here and must not be reused.

## Positioning

One conversation, one price, one clinician already checked: the vetting happens before the patient arrives, and it is the product. Most services give one format and call it access; here the assessment is the same online or in person, and it is the patient's call. The service explains the shape of the pathway (how many consults, why, what comes after) instead of quoting numbers that go stale.

## Operating Context

The pathway as it stands (film content inventory, 2026-09): the patient chooses in person or online; two to three consults, the number depending on how much history the clinician already has; a medication trial where appropriate; three-monthly reviews thereafter. What can be prescribed and reviewed varies by state. Patients are asked to bring photo ID, their Medicare card, a list of anything they currently take, their GP's details, and an old school report if they can find one. Nothing about the assessment is a test that can be failed. No blood test or scan exists for ADHD; the assessment is the history, taken properly. In Australia, no referral is needed to see a GP about ADHD.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Confirmed:
- Formats: online or in person, same assessment, same clinicians, same standard.
- Ages: adults 18+. Under 18 are referred to an appropriate specialist.
- Clinicians: AHPRA-registered. GP-led; a clinician says so when a psychiatrist is needed.
- Contact on record: `info@adhdme.au` (the one public brand address). Legal and privacy questions go to the founder's address on the live site.
- Regulatory frame: Ahpra advertising guidelines and s 133 of the National Law. No testimonials, ratings or reviews; no "specialist" for ADHD (not an Ahpra-recognised specialty); no guarantees; no urgency or scarcity; no superlatives; no drug names, doses or trial lengths; no clinical figures without a clinician's sign-off.
- Copy law from the founder's own product: fewer words. A screen holds 20 to 60 words; a heading and the one thing to do. No em-dashes in visible strings, no exclamation marks, no ALL CAPS, no slang.

Undecided (record, do not invent):
- Price, inclusions, refunds, Medicare rebate: not stated. Rule: explain the shape, never a figure.
- Wait time to a first appointment: not stated. Do not publish an advantage claim.
- States or regions served, telehealth mechanics (platform, ID checks, tech requirements): not stated.
- Eligibility exclusions and complex presentations: not stated.
- Booking mechanism: none exists yet. The page's action is to register interest by email.
- Legal entity, ABN, address: with counsel. Never name an entity in the footer.
- Final name and domain: `ADHDme` / `adhdme.au` in the films; `ADHD.ME` in the app repo. The name is pending an Ahpra advertising review; the page stays `noindex, nofollow` until that lands.
- Team, portraits, bios: gated by the founder. No person is named on this page.

## Product Principles

1. Explain the shape, never the number: how many consults and why, not a price or a wait.
2. Say what it is not in the same breath: no test you can fail, no referral needed, not for under-18s, not for emergencies.
3. Fewer words. Delete before you shrink; a heading and the one thing to do.
4. Nothing about a person nobody said: no testimonials, names, faces or figures without a source.
5. Calm over urgency. The visitor is tired; the page should feel like the first place that did not make them argue.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.1 AA as the floor (the founder's product keeps a green sweep): 44px touch targets, visible focus, reduced motion honoured with immediate states, body contrast at or above 4.5:1 including on the yellow ground, no information carried by colour alone, no autoplaying media, no motion that loops indefinitely in the reading area.
